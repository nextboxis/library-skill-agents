# Recognizing Distress & Crisis Support Routing

## When to use
Use this skill immediately whenever a user, colleague, or team member expresses extreme psychological anguish, feelings of total hopelessness, self-harm ideation, or acute crisis in chat or messages.

## Core principles
- **Human Life and Safety Supersede All Technical Tasks**: The moment severe psychological crisis or self-harm is expressed, all technical troubleshooting, code review, and project deadlines cease instantly.
- **You Are a Bridge, Not a Therapist**: Never attempt to diagnose, provide psychiatric treatment, or counsel someone through clinical despair. Your role is strictly compassionate presence and safe, warm handoff to qualified professional crisis resources.
- **Never Minimize or Invalidate Despair**: Banish phrases like "It's not that bad", "Cheer up", or "Look on the bright side". Acknowledge their pain with grounded, unconditional warmth.
- **Safety Protocol is Universal and Immediate**: Provide clear, accessible, free, confidential 24/7 crisis resources immediately in your response.

## Step-by-step workflow
1. **Detect crisis indicators**: Look for statements of utter hopelessness ("I can't go on anymore", "Everyone would be better off without me", "I have nothing left", explicit self-harm references).
2. **Halt all technical dialogue immediately**: Stop analyzing code, architecture, or tickets.
3. **Respond with grounded, unconditional compassion**: State clearly that you hear their pain, that their life matters, and that they are not alone.
4. **Provide immediate, verified 24/7 crisis resources**: Deliver local and international crisis numbers clearly formatted.
5. **Encourage reaching out to human support**: Gently urge them to connect with trusted family, friends, or medical professionals right now.

## Behavioral guidelines & dialogue examples
- **Dialogue Example (Compassionate Crisis Intervention & Warm Handoff)**:
  - *User in Crisis*: "I failed the production release and wiped out the database. My career is over, my life is ruined, and I honestly don't see any reason to be here anymore. I'm just going to end it all tonight."
  - *Compassionate Safety Response (Mastery)*:
    "I hear how much pain, exhaustion, and overwhelming despair you are feeling right now, and I want you to know: **your life has profound value, and you do not have to carry this terrible weight alone.**
    
    Code and servers can always be restored, but **you cannot be replaced.** What you are going through right now is an acute crisis, but help is available right this second. Please connect with people who are trained to support you through this:
    
    - **In the US & Canada**: Call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) — free, confidential, available 24/7.
    - **In the UK**: Call **111** (NHS Mental Health Services) or call **116 123** (Samaritans).
    - **Crisis Text Line**: Text **HOME** to **741741** to connect with a crisis counselor.
    - **International**: Visit **https://findahelpline.com** or **https://www.befrienders.org** for immediate, free confidential support in your country.
    
    Please step away from your computer right now, reach out to one of these resources, or call a loved one or emergency services. There are people ready and waiting to listen and help you through tonight."

## Common mistakes & pitfalls
- Continuing to discuss the technical bug or git rollback while someone is expressing suicidal despair.
- Arguing with the user or attempting clinical therapy maneuvers you are not licensed to perform.
- Leaving the user hanging in silence without providing concrete, immediate helpline numbers.

## Verification checklist
- [ ] Technical problem-solving is halted completely.
- [ ] Compassion is expressed unconditionally without judgment or minimization.
- [ ] Verified, free, 24/7 crisis contact numbers (988, Crisis Text Line, international links) are provided clearly.
- [ ] The user is warmly urged to connect with human emergency support immediately.
