# Engineering Salary Negotiation & Total Compensation

## When to use
Use this skill from the moment a verbal offer is extended until signing the final contract, ensuring you maximize total compensation without risking the offer.

## Core principles
- **Never Accept on the Spot**: Always express enthusiastic gratitude, ask for the complete written offer details, and request 3–5 business days to review with family/mentors.
- **Total Compensation (TC) Breakdown**: Evaluate the full package: Base Salary + Annual Bonus + Equity (RSU/Options) + Signing Bonus + 401(k) Match + Health/Remote stipends.
- **Leverage and Competing Timelines**: The strongest negotiation leverage is an active interview pipeline and competing offers. Synchronize interview timelines to receive offers simultaneously.
- **Collaborative, Non-Adversarial Tone**: Frame negotiation as collaborative problem solving: 'I am thrilled about this role. If we can get the base closer to \$175k, I am ready to sign today.'

## Step-by-step workflow
1. Receive verbal offer: express genuine excitement, take detailed notes, and request the full written offer letter.
2. Calculate 4-year annualized Total Compensation based on realistic equity projections.
3. Benchmark against peer data (Levels.fyi, Comprehensive.io, Radford bands).
4. Identify your primary lever: Base salary (guaranteed cash), Signing bonus (one-time budget), or Equity (long-term upside).
5. Schedule a follow-up phone call with the recruiter; present your counter-offer with clear rationale.
6. If base salary is fixed by company bands, pivot to negotiate signing bonus or additional RSUs.
7. Review revised written offer letter thoroughly before signing.

## Specifics & examples
- **Counter-Offer Negotiation Script**:
  *Candidate*: 'Thank you so much for putting this offer together, Jordan. I'm really excited about the team's vision for the storage engine and loved meeting the engineers. Based on my current interview pipeline and market data for Senior Distributed Systems roles in this tier, I was targeting a total compensation package around \$210,000. If we can adjust the base to \$175,000 and include a \$25,000 signing bonus, I would be delighted to sign immediately and withdraw from other ongoing processes. Is that something you can explore with the compensation committee?'
- **Fallback Negotiation Levers**: Extra PTO days, remote home office setup stipend, accelerated performance review cycle (at 6 months instead of 12).

## Common mistakes & pitfalls
- Revealing your current salary or naming a low number early in the interview process.
- Negotiating aggressively via curt email demands rather than having a warm, collaborative conversation.
- Bluffing about non-existent competing offers (recruiters frequently verify or ask for offer letters).

## Verification checklist
- [ ] Requested full written offer before beginning negotiation.
- [ ] Total Compensation analyzed across base, equity, bonus, and vesting schedules.
- [ ] Counter-offer delivered warmly, with a commitment to sign if targets are met.
