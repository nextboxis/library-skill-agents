# Section Divider Slides

## When to use
Use this skill when transitioning between major themes in long presentations (20+ slides), shifting from problem to solution, or marking phase boundaries.

## Design principles
- **Intentional Visual Contrast**: Section dividers should visually contrast with standard content slides (e.g., a dark divider slide in an otherwise light-themed deck).
- **Mental Reset & Pause**: Dividers give the audience an optical break from dense data, allowing them to re-anchor attention on the next strategic topic.
- **Oversized Section Numerals**: Use large numerals (60–96 pt) to create a striking graphic anchor that signals chapter advancement.
- **One Clear Topic Headline**: Never place body paragraphs, charts, or bullet points on a section divider; keep it strictly minimal.

## Step-by-step workflow
1. **Identify natural transition points**: Insert dividers only at major thematic boundaries (every 5–8 content slides).
2. **Apply contrasting background**: If the deck is primarily light (#FFFFFF), format divider slides with the primary brand color (#1E3A8A) or dark slate (#0F172A).
3. **Position oversized section number**: Place the chapter number (`01`, `02`, `03`) in 72–96 pt bold type as a graphic focal point.
4. **Position chapter title**: Place the section title in 36–44 pt bold text directly adjacent to or beneath the number.
5. **Add optional one-sentence premise**: Include a brief 16–18 pt italicized subtitle framing what this section resolves.

## Specifics & examples
- **Dark Divider Slide Specifications**:
  - Background: `#0F172A` (Slate 900).
  - Chapter Eyebrow: `left = 1.2 in`, `top = 2.4 in`, `font = 14 pt Semi-Bold #38BDF8` ("SECTION 02").
  - Section Main Title: `left = 1.2 in`, `top = 2.8 in`, `width = 10.0 in`, `font = 44 pt Bold #FFFFFF`.
  - Premise Line: `left = 1.2 in`, `top = 4.2 in`, `width = 8.5 in`, `font = 18 pt Regular #94A3B8`.
- **Minimalist Light Divider Alternative**:
  - Background: `#F8FAFC`.
  - Massive Number: `left = 1.0 in`, `top = 2.2 in`, `font = 96 pt Bold #E2E8F0` (Watermark style).
  - Title Overlay: `left = 1.2 in`, `top = 3.6 in`, `font = 40 pt Bold #0F172A`.

## Common mistakes
- Placing bullet points, graphs, or data tables on divider slides, ruining their function as a visual pause.
- Inserting a divider every 2 slides, destroying the momentum of the presentation.
- Keeping divider slides visually identical to normal content slides, making transitions unnoticeable.

## Review checklist
- [ ] Divider slide provides immediate visual contrast against preceding content slides.
- [ ] Section title is large (36–44 pt), bold, and unambiguous.
- [ ] Slide is completely free of body text, bullet points, and data charts.
- [ ] Chapter number clearly reflects the sequence outlined in the initial agenda.
