# LinkedIn Profile Optimization for Software Engineers

## When to use
Use this skill when conducting an active or passive job search, positioning yourself for inbound recruiter messages and executive searches.

## Core principles
- **Recruiter Search Indexing (SEO)**: Recruiters search LinkedIn using boolean filters (`"Distributed Systems" AND ("Go" OR "Golang") AND "Kubernetes"`). Embed target skills naturally throughout your profile.
- **Headline as Value Proposition**: Your headline follows you across every comment and search result. Never leave it as just 'Software Engineer at Company'. State your specialization, core tech, and scale.
- **First-Person Storytelling in 'About'**: Use the About section to convey your engineering philosophy, what problems excite you, and what systems you specialize in building.
- **Featured Section Proof of Work**: Pin your best GitHub case studies, conference talk recordings, or technical blog articles at the top of your profile.

## Step-by-step workflow
1. Craft headline: `[Role/Specialization] | [Core Tech Stack] | [Scale or Passion]`.
2. Add high-resolution professional headshot and clean technical banner image (architecture sketch, terminal, or minimal geometric).
3. Write 3-paragraph About section: 1) Engineering background & passion, 2) Core technical competencies, 3) Projects & personal interests.
4. Populate Experience bullets using quantified XYZ formulas.
5. Pin 3 top technical assets in the Featured section with clickable URLs.
6. Populate all 50 Skills slots, ensuring top 3 reflect your primary desired job title.
7. Toggle 'Open to Work' with visibility set to 'Recruiters only' if currently employed.

## Specifics & examples
- **High-Conversion Headline Examples**:
  - *Backend*: 'Senior Backend Engineer | Go, Distributed Systems, Kafka | Scaling APIs to 50k+ QPS'
  - *Frontend*: 'Staff Frontend Architect | React, TypeScript, Next.js, Design Systems | Web Performance Obsessive'
  - *DevOps*: 'Cloud Infrastructure Engineer | AWS, Terraform, Kubernetes | Multi-Region Resiliency & FinOps'
- **Featured Section Link Template**: Link to GitHub repository with custom thumbnail graphic.

## Common mistakes & pitfalls
- Leaving the default headline 'Student at University' or 'Software Engineer at Steath Startup'.
- Setting 'Open to Work' public green banner when attempting to keep a job search confidential from current employers.
- Leaving the About section completely blank.

## Verification checklist
- [ ] Headline states specialization, tech stack, and scale.
- [ ] About section tells an authentic first-person engineering story.
- [ ] Featured section links directly to live projects, GitHub repos, or technical writing.
