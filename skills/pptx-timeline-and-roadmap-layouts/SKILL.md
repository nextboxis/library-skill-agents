# Timeline and Roadmap Slide Layouts

## When to use
Use this skill when communicating strategic roadmaps, project phases, quarterly milestones, historical retrospectives, or multi-year transformation plans.

## Design principles
- **Horizontal Left-to-Right Progression**: Time flows naturally from left to right; never stack chronological phases vertically on a widescreen slide.
- **The Central Spine Rule**: Anchor chronological points with a horizontal timeline spine line (`#CBD5E1`, 2–3 pt stroke) across the slide center.
- **Alternating or Uniform Nodes**: Position milestone cards either uniformly above/below the spine or alternating to maximize vertical breathing room.
- **Date Prominence**: Make milestone dates or quarter tags (e.g., "Q1 2026", "Month 3") distinct using bold badges or saturated accent text.

## Step-by-step workflow
1. **Determine phase granularity**: Cap timeline events between 3 and 5 milestones per slide to maintain legibility.
2. **Draw baseline spine**: Insert a horizontal connector or line across the canvas at `top = 3.6 in`, spanning from `left = 1.0 in` to `left = 12.333 in`.
3. **Distribute node markers**: Place circular milestone badges (0.35 x 0.35 in) along the spine at mathematically equal increments.
4. **Position content cards**: Place milestone cards with quarter label, headline, and 2 bullet deliverables directly under each node.
5. **Highlight current phase**: Style the active phase with a vibrant brand fill (`#2563EB`) and subdued past/future phases (`#94A3B8`).

## Specifics & examples
- **4-Quarter Strategic Roadmap Geometry (16:9 Canvas)**:
  - Usable Width: 11.733 in (`left = 0.8 in` to `12.533 in`).
  - 4 Milestone Cards: Width = 2.683 in each, Gutters = 0.333 in.
  - Card 1: `left = 0.800 in`, Card 2: `left = 3.816 in`, Card 3: `left = 6.832 in`, Card 4: `left = 9.848 in`.
  - Spine Line: `top = 2.4 in`, `height = 0 in`, `stroke = 3 pt #E2E8F0`.
  - Milestone Badges: `top = 2.225 in`, `width = 0.35 in`, `height = 0.35 in`, rounded shape `#2563EB`.
  - Date Text: `14 pt Bold #2563EB` above spine; Card Title: `16 pt Bold #0F172A` below spine; Bullets: `13 pt Regular #475569`.

## Common mistakes
- Squeezing 8 to 10 milestones into a single slide, turning milestone descriptions into unreadable clutter.
- Stacking timeline milestones in a serpentine or zig-zag pattern that violates natural scanning order.
- Omitting clear visual indicators for the current date or completed milestones.

## Review checklist
- [ ] Timeline contains between 3 and 5 milestones evenly spaced across the canvas.
- [ ] Time progression moves strictly from left to right.
- [ ] Horizontal spine connects nodes cleanly without misalignment.
- [ ] Current or upcoming milestone is visually emphasized over historical phases.
