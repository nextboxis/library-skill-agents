# Action Titles and Takeaways

## When to use
Use this skill when drafting or reviewing slide headers across an entire presentation to ensure every slide communicates a decisive conclusion.

## Design principles
- **Lead with the Conclusion**: Replace passive category labels (e.g., "Revenue Analysis") with active business conclusions (e.g., "Enterprise Subscriptions Drove 64% of Q4 Revenue").
- **Two-Tier Headline Architecture**:
  - Tier 1: Action Title (28–36 pt Bold, 1–2 lines max).
  - Tier 2: Context Subtitle / Tracker (16–18 pt Regular muted gray).
- **The Skim Test**: Reading only the slide titles in sequence must convey the complete executive story with zero ambiguity.
- **Subject-Verb-Impact Construction**: Structure action titles with an active subject, a strong verb, and a measurable impact.

## Step-by-step workflow
1. **Audit existing slide titles**: Identify passive topic labels like "Agenda", "Overview", "Architecture", "Market Trends", or "Next Steps".
2. **Identify slide's primary finding**: Ask: "If the audience remembers only one sentence from this slide, what must it be?"
3. **Formulate active sentence**:
   - Passive: "User Retention Rates".
   - Active: "90-Day Retention Increased to 74% Following Onboarding Redesign".
4. **Position headline container**:
   - `left = 0.8 in`, `top = 0.6 in`, `width = 11.733 in`, `height = 1.0 in`.
5. **Verify line wrapping**: Ensure the action title wraps to a maximum of 2 lines; never allow a 3-line headline.

## Specifics & examples
- **Action Title Transformation Matrix**:
  - *Passive*: "Security Operations Metrics" -> *Active*: "Autonomous Agent Triage Reduced Mean Time to Remediation by 45%".
  - *Passive*: "Cloud Infrastructure Costs" -> *Active*: "Migrating to Antigravity Reduced Monthly Cluster Spend by \$18,000".
  - *Passive*: "Customer Feedback Summary" -> *Active*: "88% of Beta Customers Rated the Unified Workspace 'Indispensable'".
  - *Passive*: "Q1 2026 Roadmap" -> *Active*: "Phase 1 Cutover Delivers Automated Rollbacks by February 28".

## Common mistakes
- Using single-word titles ("Marketing", "Sales", "Challenges", "Strategy") that convey zero information.
- Writing action titles so long (4+ lines) that they push slide content off the bottom of the canvas.
- Stating a claim in the action title that is contradicted or unsupported by the chart on the slide.

## Review checklist
- [ ] Slide title is a complete, declarative sentence stating the core finding.
- [ ] Title passes the Skim Test: Reading titles sequentially delivers the full presentation pitch.
- [ ] Title wraps to at most 2 lines in 28–36 pt bold text.
- [ ] Every claim made in the title is directly substantiated by the slide body elements.
