# Invoicing, Late Payments & Freelance Financial Hygiene

## When to use
Use this skill to maintain healthy freelance cash flow, prevent payment delays, and handle delinquent clients professionally.

## Core principles
- **Upfront Deposits are Non-Negotiable**: Never begin work without a 30% to 50% upfront deposit cleared in your bank account.
- **Strict Payment Terms (Net-15)**: Avoid Net-60 or Net-90 terms. Enforce Net-14 or Net-15 payment terms with automatic 1.5% monthly late interest penalties stated in the contract.
- **Stoppage of Work Clause**: If an invoice is overdue by 7 business days, all development and server support halts immediately until balance is settled.
- **Automated Invoicing Systems**: Use professional accounting software (FreshBooks, QuickBooks, Stripe Invoicing) that automatically sends polite reminder sequences.

## Step-by-step workflow
1. Issue invoice immediately upon milestone completion with exact itemized deliverables, payment terms, and direct ACH/wire details.
2. Send automated reminder 3 days before due date ('Friendly heads-up on Invoice #104').
3. If unpaid on due date: send Day 1 overdue notice politely inquiring if payment was initiated.
4. Day 7 overdue: send formal notice invoking the 'Stoppage of Work' clause and pausing development.
5. Day 14 overdue: initiate a phone call to the client's accounting/finance department.
6. Day 30 overdue: issue formal Demand Letter with late penalty fees accrued.

## Specifics & examples
- **Overdue Payment Escalation Sequence**:
  - *Day 1 Overdue*: 'Hi Sarah, I noticed Invoice #104 was due yesterday. Could you let me know when we can expect the ACH transfer to clear? Let me know if you need another copy of the invoice.'
  - *Day 7 Overdue (Work Stoppage)*: 'Hi Sarah, as Invoice #104 is now 7 days past due, our agreement requires pausing active development on the payment module until the account is brought current. Once payment is confirmed, we will gladly resume work immediately.'
- **Bank Account Separation**: Maintain separate business checking, tax savings (reserve 30% of every dollar), and personal accounts.

## Common mistakes & pitfalls
- Continuing to work for a client who is weeks behind on previous invoices.
- Sending messy invoices via Word docs without invoice numbers or payment terms.
- Failing to set aside 30% of incoming revenue for quarterly estimated income taxes.

## Verification checklist
- [ ] Invoices clearly state Net-14/Net-15 terms and automated late penalty rate.
- [ ] Work stoppage clause is enforced when invoices exceed grace periods.
- [ ] 30% of every invoice receipt is segregated immediately into a tax reserve account.
