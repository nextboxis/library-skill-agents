# The 60-30-10 Color Rule for Slides

## When to use
Use this skill when coloring multi-element slides, charts, and card layouts to maintain visual harmony and ensure key takeaways stand out instantly.

## Design principles
- **60% Dominant Canvas**: The majority of the slide surface area is occupied by the neutral background, providing breathing room.
- **30% Structural Elements**: Cards, secondary text, table borders, and structural shapes provide architecture and organization.
- **10% Intentional Accent**: A single vivid color applied strictly to the most critical takeaway, hero metric, or active timeline stage.
- **Accent Exclusivity**: If everything is highlighted, nothing is highlighted. The 10% accent must be applied with severe discipline.

## Step-by-step workflow
1. **Lock the 60% base**: Fill the slide canvas with neutral white (`#FFFFFF`) or dark slate (`#0F172A`).
2. **Apply the 30% structure**: Format content containers, cards, and secondary text using structural neutrals (`#F8FAFC` cards, `#475569` text).
3. **Identify the 10% target**: Pinpoint the single number, column, or card that represents the core takeaway.
4. **Deploy the accent**: Apply the vibrant accent color (`#2563EB` or `#059669`) exclusively to that target element.
5. **Audit balance**: Check that the accent does not bleed into background elements or non-critical labels.

## Specifics & examples
- **Visual Distribution Example (3-Column Pricing / Strategy Slide)**:
  - **60% Dominant**: Clean canvas background (`#FFFFFF`).
  - **30% Structural**: 2 standard option cards in subtle grey fill (`#F8FAFC`, border `#E2E8F0`), with neutral dark text (`#0F172A`).
  - **10% Accent**: The recommended "Enterprise" middle card features an accent banner (`#2563EB`), bold blue price metric, and blue button.
- **Data Chart Distribution**:
  - 60% Canvas: White background.
  - 30% Structural: 4 baseline comparison bars in soft grey (`#CBD5E1`).
  - 10% Accent: The current year's winning bar in vibrant blue (`#2563EB`).

## Common mistakes
- Applying the accent color to all card backgrounds, reversing the ratio to 60% accent and blinding the viewer.
- Using 3 different accent colors across a single slide.
- Forgetting structural neutral tones and using raw primary colors for container borders.

## Review checklist
- [ ] Background represents roughly 60% of the visible surface area.
- [ ] Structural containers and neutral text represent roughly 30%.
- [ ] Vibrant accent color occupies no more than 10% of the visual field.
- [ ] The accent color points directly to the primary takeaway or decision item.
