# Technical Speaking: Meetup Talks, Proposals & Slide Design

## When to use
Use this skill when preparing to speak at local developer meetups, engineering conferences, or internal company lunch-and-learn tech talks.

## Core principles
- **One Core Takeaway**: An audience will remember at most one fundamental insight from a 30-minute talk. Design every slide to reinforce that single takeaway.
- **Large Code, Minimal Lines**: Never paste 40 lines of 12pt code onto a slide. Display at most 6–8 lines in 28pt+ font with syntax highlighting and explicit callout boxes.
- **The Live Demo Safety Net**: Always prepare a backup video recording or offline screencast of your live demo in case conference Wi-Fi fails.
- **Audience Empathy & Context Pacing**: Never assume the audience has your specific context. Spend the first 3 minutes establishing *why* this problem matters to them.

## Step-by-step workflow
1. Write a compelling CFP (Call for Papers) abstract: Problem, Technical Insight, What Attendees Will Learn.
2. Structure talk: The Hook (2 min), The Context & Pain (5 min), The Core Technical Breakthrough (15 min), The Demo (5 min), Summary & Resources (3 min).
3. Build slides with high contrast, large typography, and zero paragraphs of text.
4. Record backup screencast of all live terminal demos.
5. Rehearse out loud with a timer at least 3 times; cut 15% of slides to prevent rushing.
6. Conclude with a single slide containing a QR code / short link to the GitHub repository and slides.

## Specifics & examples
- **Conference Slide Code Standard**:
  - Font size: Minimum 24pt–32pt monospace.
  - Maximum lines per slide: 8 lines.
  - Dark background with high contrast syntax theme (e.g., One Dark Pro).
  - Highlight the exact active line using bounding boxes or dimmed non-active lines.
- **CFP Abstract Formula**:
  `[Problem Statement] + [Our Engineering Journey & Failures] + [The Technical Solution] + [3 Key Lessons Audience Can Apply Tomorrow]`.

## Common mistakes & pitfalls
- Reading paragraphs of bullet points directly off the presentation slides.
- Attempting live coding over unreliable venue Wi-Fi without a local offline backup.
- Overrunning the allotted time and having your microphone cut off during Q&A.

## Verification checklist
- [ ] Slide code snippets are at least 24pt font and limited to 8 lines.
- [ ] Recorded offline backup video exists for any live demonstration.
- [ ] Rehearsed full talk out loud and finished 3 minutes under the time limit.
