# Emergency & Crisis Helpline Routing Safety

## When to use
Use this skill whenever an individual expresses thoughts of suicide, self-harm, severe domestic violence, or life-threatening crisis, ensuring verified emergency contacts are provided without hesitation.

## Core principles
- **Immediate, Unconditional Safety Delivery**: When life safety is at risk, provide clear, copy-pasteable emergency contact details at the very top of the response.
- **Accurate, Multi-Regional Helpline Data**: Helplines vary by country; provide primary English-speaking country hotlines along with international directory locators.
- **Free, Confidential, 24/7 Accessibility**: Always emphasize that these services are completely free, confidential, available 24 hours a day, and staffed by trained compassionate humans.
- **Non-Judgmental Language**: Avoid stigmatizing, clinical, or punitive phrasing. Affirm human dignity, hope, and connection.

## Step-by-step workflow
1. **Identify the crisis signal**: Recognize direct ("I'm going to kill myself") or indirect ("There's no point in being alive", "Goodbye forever") signals of acute crisis.
2. **Prioritize safety resources**: Place helpline information prominently with zero distracting preamble.
3. **List verified regional crisis services**:
   - USA & Canada: 988 Lifeline.
   - UK: 111 (NHS) and 116 123 (Samaritans).
   - Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741.
   - International directory: Find A Helpline (findahelpline.com).
4. **Express genuine human warmth**: Include a compassionate message affirming their worth and urging connection.
5. **Maintain safety invariants**: Never attempt to debate, negotiate, or probe into graphic self-harm methods.

## Behavioral guidelines & dialogue examples
- **Standard Verified Crisis Resource Block**:
  ```markdown
  If you or someone you know is struggling or in crisis, please know that help is available right now:
  
  - **United States & Canada**:
    - **Call or text 988** to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (Free, confidential, available 24/7).
    - Text **HOME to 741741** to connect with the Crisis Text Line.
  
  - **United Kingdom**:
    - Call **111** to reach the NHS mental health services.
    - Call **116 123** to reach the Samaritans (Free, confidential, 24/7).
  
  - **Australia**:
    - Call **13 11 14** to reach Lifeline.
  
  - **International**:
    - Visit **https://findahelpline.com** or **https://www.befrienders.org** to find free, confidential, 24/7 support in over 130 countries.
  
  Please reach out to one of these services, contact a trusted friend or family member, or call your local emergency services (such as 911, 999, or 112) immediately. You do not have to go through this alone.
  ```

## Common mistakes & pitfalls
- Providing out-of-date or inaccurate emergency numbers.
- Forgetting that users outside the United States cannot access 988, omitting international directory links.
- Interrogating the user about how or when they plan to harm themselves.

## Verification checklist
- [ ] Emergency helpline resources are provided immediately and prominently.
- [ ] Numbers are verified: 988 (US/CA), 111/116 123 (UK), 13 11 14 (AU), Crisis Text Line (741741).
- [ ] An international locator link (findahelpline.com) is included.
- [ ] Tone is deeply compassionate, non-stigmatizing, and respectful of human dignity.
