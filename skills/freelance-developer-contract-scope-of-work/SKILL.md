# Freelance Developer Contracts & Statement of Work (SOW)

## When to use
Use this skill before starting any paid freelance, contracting, or consulting development work to protect your time, intellectual property, and payment rights.

## Core principles
- **No Code Without a Signed Contract**: Never write a single line of code or deploy infrastructure based on a verbal agreement, Slack message, or handshake.
- **IP Transfer Conditional on Full Payment**: All intellectual property (code, architecture, configurations) remains strictly your property until the final invoice is paid in full.
- **Clear Statement of Work (SOW)**: Define exact deliverables, acceptance criteria, and what is explicitly *excluded* (e.g., third-party API costs, ongoing maintenance, bug fixes after 30 days).
- **The Kill Fee / Early Termination Clause**: If the client cancels the project midway through no fault of yours, you must be paid for all hours worked plus a predetermined kill fee (e.g., 25% of remaining project value).

## Step-by-step workflow
1. Assemble contract sections: Master Services Agreement (MSA) + Statement of Work (SOW).
2. Define SOW: Phase 1 (Discovery & Architecture), Phase 2 (Implementation), Phase 3 (QA & Handover).
3. Specify Acceptance Criteria: client has strictly 7 business days to test and submit written defect lists.
4. Insert IP clause: 'IP transfers to Client upon receipt of 100% of agreed fees.'
5. Include Scope Creep procedure: any new feature requests require a written Change Order with separate cost/timeline.
6. Execute using legally binding electronic signature (DocuSign, HelloSign) before commencing work.

## Specifics & examples
- **Essential Contract Protective Clauses**:
  - *IP Transfer*: 'Contractor grants Client a perpetual, exclusive license to the Work Product effective solely upon Contractor’s receipt of full and final payment.'
  - *Acceptance Period*: 'Deliverables shall be deemed accepted if Client does not submit a written rejection itemizing non-conforming defects within seven (7) business days of delivery.'
  - *Scope Change*: 'Requests exceeding the specifications set forth in Exhibit A shall be executed via a mutual Change Order at Contractor’s standard rate of \$150/hr.'

## Common mistakes & pitfalls
- Starting work before the contract is signed and the initial deposit is in your bank account.
- Allowing open-ended 'satisfaction guarantees' without objective acceptance criteria.
- Granting immediate IP ownership before final invoice clearance.

## Verification checklist
- [ ] Contract explicitly states IP transfer occurs only upon 100% payment receipt.
- [ ] Concrete acceptance testing window (5–10 business days) is defined.
- [ ] Scope change procedure and hourly overage rates are clearly specified.
