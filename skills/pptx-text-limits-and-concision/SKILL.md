# Text Limits and Concision for Slides

## When to use
Use this skill when reviewing draft decks overflowing with paragraphs, preparing slides for executive leadership, or coaching presenters to stop reading their slides.

## Design principles
- **Slides are Visual Anchors, Not Teleprompters**: The audience cannot read paragraphs and listen to the speaker simultaneously. Text must be telegraphic.
- **The 6x6 Rule (Maximum Ceiling)**: Never exceed 6 bullet points per slide, and never exceed 6 words per bullet point for keynote/stage slides.
- **Bold Lead-in Formatting**: Begin bullet points with a 2–3 word bold takeaway keyword, followed by a concise explanatory clause.
- **Metric Replacement**: Replace 25 words of descriptive text with a single bold number and a 3-word label whenever possible.

## Step-by-step workflow
1. **Count total slide words**: Flag any slide with >75 words (for presentation decks) or >140 words (for read-ahead memos).
2. **Extract active verbs and nouns**: Highlight the essential business claims; eliminate passive auxiliary verbs and filler adverbs.
3. **Apply bold lead-in pattern**: Structure points as: `**[Bold Concept]**: [Succinct impact statement]`.
4. **Convert lists to visual cards**: When a list has 3 items, convert it into 3 horizontal container cards with bold metrics.
5. **Relocate details to Speaker Notes**: Cut extended explanations and paste them directly into the slide's speaker notes field.

## Specifics & examples
- **Before (Wordy & Unfocused - 42 Words)**:
  - "Our technical team has spent the last three quarters working on various performance enhancements across our distributed database layer, which has ultimately resulted in a noticeable reduction in average API response times for our end customers of roughly 45%."
- **After (Concise, High-Impact - 9 Words)**:
  - **45% Faster API Latency**: Database refactoring completed across Q1–Q3.
- **Standard Word Budget Limits**:
  - Live Keynote / Stage Presentation: 25–40 words max per slide.
  - Executive Briefing / Board Review: 50–80 words max per slide.
  - Read-Ahead Technical Memo Deck: 100–140 words max per slide.

## Common mistakes
- Pasting entire email threads or formal report paragraphs directly into bullet points.
- Writing complete grammatical sentences with multiple dependent clauses on presentation slides.
- Leaving bullet points that wrap across 4 lines of text.

## Review checklist
- [ ] Slide contains fewer than 60 words (presentation) or 120 words (read-ahead).
- [ ] Bullet points use bold lead-in keywords for scannability.
- [ ] Passive sentences are converted into active, metric-driven claims.
- [ ] Detailed speaker scripts are stored in the Speaker Notes pane.
