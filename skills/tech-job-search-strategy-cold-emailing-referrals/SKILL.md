# Tech Job Search Strategy: Cold Outreach & Referrals

## When to use
Use this skill when applying to competitive engineering roles to bypass the 'black hole' of public job boards and land direct interviews.

## Core principles
- **The Internal Referral Advantage**: Applications submitted with an internal employee referral are 10x more likely to secure a first-round interview than blind portal submissions.
- **Personalized Value over Spray-and-Pray**: Sending 10 deeply researched, personalized cold emails achieves a higher conversion rate than submitting 200 generic 1-click LinkedIn applications.
- **Targeting Engineering Managers Directly**: Reach out to the Engineering Manager or Director who has the headcount and budget, rather than overloaded generic HR inboxes.
- **The 3-Sentence Outreach Rule**: Keep cold outreach under 100 words: who you are, why you specifically admire their team's engineering work, and how your experience solves their exact hiring need.

## Step-by-step workflow
1. Build a target company list of 20–30 organizations matching your technical interests and values.
2. Identify the specific Engineering Manager or Team Lead on LinkedIn.
3. Find verified work email using professional lookup tools or company domain patterns (`firstname.lastname@company.com`).
4. Review their recent public talks, engineering blog posts, or open-source PRs.
5. Send a concise 3-paragraph cold email with your portfolio/GitHub link attached.
6. Send a polite, 2-sentence follow-up 4 business days later if no response.

## Specifics & examples
- **High-Conversion Cold Email Template**:
  *Subject*: Question regarding your recent post on Kafka lag / Staff Backend role

  *Hi Maya,*,

  *I read your recent engineering blog post on how your team resolved consumer group rebalances in Kafka—the approach of batching commits using custom offsets was brilliant.*

  *I'm a backend engineer with 4 years of experience scaling event-driven pipelines in Go and Kafka. At [PreviousCompany], I architected a stream processor handling 20k events/sec with sub-50ms latency.*

  *I noticed your team is hiring a Senior Distributed Systems Engineer. Here is my [Portfolio/GitHub] with architecture case studies. Would you be open to a 10-minute chat next week to see if my background aligns with your team's road map?*

  *Best regards, Alex Chen*

## Common mistakes & pitfalls
- Pasting a 500-word wall of text copying your entire resume into the email.
- Sending the exact same generic template to 50 managers with mismatched company names.
- Demanding a referral from a stranger on LinkedIn without providing any context or rapport.

## Verification checklist
- [ ] Cold email is strictly under 150 words and customized to a specific engineering accomplishment.
- [ ] Addressed to the relevant Engineering Manager or Team Lead.
- [ ] Includes direct clickable link to GitHub portfolio or case study.
