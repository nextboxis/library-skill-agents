# Freelance Pricing: Hourly, Milestone & Value-Based Rates

## When to use
Use this skill when quoting project fees, sending proposals to prospective clients, or transitioning from hourly billing to high-margin fixed pricing.

## Core principles
- **The Freelance Overhead Factor**: Your freelance hourly rate must be at least 2.5x to 3x your salaried equivalent to cover self-employment tax, healthcare, unbilled business development, and equipment.
- **Hourly vs Fixed Milestone**: Hourly penalizes speed (the faster you work, the less you earn). Fixed milestone pricing rewards efficiency and deep expertise.
- **Value-Based Pricing**: Price according to the business value delivered to the client, not the hours spent typing. If a \$15,000 integration saves the client \$200,000 annually, the ROI is overwhelming.
- **The Minimum Project Floor**: Establish a minimum project engagement fee (e.g., \$3,000 or \$5,000) to filter out high-maintenance, low-budget clients.

## Step-by-step workflow
1. Calculate your baseline hourly floor: `(Desired Annual Salary + Business Expenses) / 1,000 billable hours`.
2. Uncover client business value during discovery: 'What is the cost of this bug/outage to your business each month?'
3. Choose pricing model: Hourly for open-ended R&D; Milestone/Fixed for well-defined deliverables; Retainer for ongoing advisory.
4. Provide 3 tiered pricing options in the proposal (Anchor, Target, Premium).
5. Require a non-refundable upfront deposit (e.g., 50% deposit before start, 25% midway, 25% upon completion).
6. Present pricing with confidence; never apologize for professional rates.

## Specifics & examples
- **Hourly Floor Calculation Example**:
  - Desired Take-Home: \$120,000
  - Taxes (30%) + Overhead/Health (\$20,000) = \$170,000 Total Needed
  - Realistic Billable Hours/Year: 1,000 hours (20 hrs/week x 50 weeks)
  - Minimum Hourly Rate Floor = \$170,000 / 1,000 = \$170/hour.
- **3-Tier Proposal Model**:
  - *Option 1 (Core)*: Backend API & Database Setup (\$6,000).
  - *Option 2 (Recommended)*: Core + Stripe Billing + Admin Dashboard (\$9,500).
  - *Option 3 (Full VIP)*: Option 2 + 30 Days Priority Support + CI/CD Setup (\$14,000).

## Common mistakes & pitfalls
- Pricing based on 40 billable hours per week (freelancers spend 40–50% of time on marketing, sales, and admin).
- Negotiating against yourself by lowering prices before the client even objects.
- Starting fixed-price projects with ambiguous, open-ended requirements.

## Verification checklist
- [ ] Rate accounts for self-employment taxes, healthcare, and non-billable hours.
- [ ] Proposes tiered pricing options anchored against business value.
- [ ] Mandatory 30%–50% upfront deposit required before project kickoff.
