# Whitespace and Breathing Room

## When to use
Use this skill when reviewing draft slides packed with too much text, tables, or icons, and when restructuring content to create an executive, uncluttered feel.

## Design principles
- **Whitespace is an Active Element**: Empty space is not wasted space; it directs eye movement, frames takeaways, and signals confidence.
- **Internal vs. External Padding**: Space between unrelated elements must always exceed space between related sub-elements.
- **Card Breathing Room**: Container cards require at least 0.3 in internal padding so text never touches container borders.
- **30–40% Negative Space Target**: A well-designed executive slide typically leaves 30% to 40% of its total canvas unencumbered by ink.

## Step-by-step workflow
1. **Audit slide density**: Calculate approximate ink-to-whitespace ratio; flag any slide where text and shapes cover >70% of canvas.
2. **Prune redundant elements**: Delete decorative line dividers, background gradients, redundant icons, and filler prose.
3. **Enforce macro margins**: Verify that the top title is separated from content cards by at least 0.5 in of vertical buffer.
4. **Expand micro margins**: Increase card internal padding and line spacing (1.2x) to give text room to breathe.
5. **Split overcrowded slides**: If elements cannot fit with generous whitespace, split into two sequential slides.

## Specifics & examples
- **Canvas Size**: 16:9 widescreen (13.333 in × 7.500 in).
- **Clearance Guidelines**:
  - Title to Content Gutter: >= 0.50 in (e.g., Title ends at `1.40 in`, content starts at `1.90 in`).
  - Card Internal Padding: `0.30 in` to `0.40 in` on all four edges.
  - Inter-card Horizontal Gap: `0.35 in` to `0.50 in`.
  - Bottom Footer Clearance: >= 0.60 in from bottom edge.
- **Reduction Metric**: Aim for a maximum of 40–60 words per slide for live talks, and 80–120 words for read-ahead decks.

## Common mistakes
- Filling every corner of the slide with logos, decorative flourishes, and sidebars.
- Squeezing 5 columns into a space that comfortably holds 3.
- Reducing font size to 10 pt just to fit an entire memo onto one slide.

## Review checklist
- [ ] At least 30% of the slide canvas is intentional negative space.
- [ ] Text inside containers has at least 0.3 in padding from card borders.
- [ ] Clear vertical separation (>= 0.5 in) separates the title from the content zone.
- [ ] The slide can be read comfortably without feeling overwhelming.
