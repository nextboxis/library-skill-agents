# One Message Per Slide

## When to use
Use this skill when an author attempts to cram strategy, timeline, architecture, and financials onto a single slide, or when an audience struggles to identify the core point.

## Design principles
- **Cognitive Load Minimization**: The human brain can only process one complex concept at a time during live spoken presentations.
- **Action Title as Thesis**: The slide headline must state the single core takeaway; all content below must strictly support that single thesis.
- **Slides are Free**: Splitting one crowded slide into three clean, focused slides costs nothing and dramatically increases executive retention.
- **Eliminate Competing Narratives**: If a slide requires saying "Now looking at the other side of the slide...", it contains two slides.

## Step-by-step workflow
1. **Extract all claims**: Read the slide and list every distinct assertion or conclusion being made.
2. **Identify the primary decision point**: Ask: "If the audience only remembers one sentence from this slide, what must it be?"
3. **Decouple secondary themes**: Move supporting topics (e.g., deep technical specs, extended timelines) to dedicated follow-on slides.
4. **Rewrite title as action takeaway**: Replace generic labels like "Project Status" with the core takeaway: "Core Migration Completed Ahead of Schedule".
5. **Prune non-supporting elements**: Remove any chart, bullet, or metric that does not directly prove the action title.

## Specifics & examples
- **Before (Crowded, Multi-message)**:
  - Title: "Q3 Operations and Technology Overview"
  - Content: 4 bullets on logistics + 1 pie chart on cloud spend + 1 roadmap timeline + 2 callouts on HR hiring.
- **After (Deconstructed into 3 Focused Slides)**:
  - Slide 1: "Logistics Optimization Reduced Delivery Times by 18%" (Hero KPI + 3 supporting operational metrics).
  - Slide 2: "Cloud Infrastructure Costs Stabilized at \$1.2M Run-Rate" (Clean bar chart comparing Q1–Q3 spend).
  - Slide 3: "Engineering Hiring Remains on Track for Q4 Product Launch" (Clean 3-stage milestone roadmap).

## Common mistakes
- Keeping content on one slide because "it all relates to the project".
- Writing a broad category title ("Financial Overview") because the slide lacks a clear single message.
- Shrinking text to unreadable sizes just to keep secondary data on the same slide.

## Review checklist
- [ ] The slide communicates exactly one decisive conclusion.
- [ ] The action title clearly articulates that single conclusion.
- [ ] Every element on the canvas directly supports or proves the title thesis.
- [ ] Secondary topics are moved to separate, dedicated slides.
