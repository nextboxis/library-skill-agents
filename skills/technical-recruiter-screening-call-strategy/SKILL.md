# Technical Recruiter Screening Call Strategy

## When to use
Use this skill at the first stage of any hiring process when speaking with an internal technical recruiter or talent acquisition specialist.

## Core principles
- **Recruiter as Internal Ally**: The recruiter wants you to pass; their job performance is measured by successful hires. Treat them as a supportive partner, not an adversary.
- **The 2-Minute Elevator Pitch**: Have a crisp, chronological narrative explaining: 1) where you started, 2) major technical milestones, 3) why this specific company and role is the natural next step.
- **Salary Range Anchoring**: Avoid giving an exact number too early. Deflect gracefully: 'I'm focused on finding the right technical fit right now. What is the approved salary band for this role?'
- **Showing High Intent**: Asking 2 thoughtful questions about team growth, engineering culture, or deployment practices shows that you are selective and genuinely interested.

## Step-by-step workflow
1. Research company mission, recent funding/product launches, and tech stack beforehand.
2. Practice the 2-minute 'Walk me through your resume' response out loud.
3. Answer 'Why us?': cite a specific product feature, engineering blog post, or open-source tool they maintain.
4. When asked about compensation: inquire about their budgeted range first before anchoring.
5. Ask 2 sharp questions: 'What are the top technical challenges the team is facing this quarter?' and 'What separates good hires from exceptional hires on this team?'
6. Send a brief thank-you email within 2 hours reiterating excitement.

## Specifics & examples
- **Graceful Salary Deflection Scripts**:
  - *Recruiter*: 'What are your current salary expectations?'
  - *Candidate*: 'Since total compensation can vary widely depending on equity, bonus structures, and benefits, I'd prefer to understand the role and meet the engineering team before settling on a specific number. What is the budgeted salary band for this position?'
- **Post-Call Thank You Email**:
  *Subject*: Thank you - [Your Name] / [Role Title]
  *Body*: 'Hi Sarah, thank you for sharing more about [Company]'s migration to Kubernetes today. Our conversation reinforced my excitement about the team's mission. Looking forward to the next steps!'

## Common mistakes & pitfalls
- Rambling for 10 minutes on the 'tell me about yourself' question without pausing.
- Badmouthing a past manager or current employer when asked why you are looking to leave.
- Having zero questions prepared when the recruiter asks 'What questions do you have for me?'

## Verification checklist
- [ ] Delivered crisp 2-minute career trajectory narrative without rambling.
- [ ] Handled salary inquiry professionally by asking for the budgeted band.
- [ ] Prepared 2 thoughtful questions regarding engineering culture and team roadmap.
