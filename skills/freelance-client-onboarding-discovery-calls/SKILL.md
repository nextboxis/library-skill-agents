# Freelance Client Onboarding & Technical Discovery Calls

## When to use
Use this skill during the initial 30-to-45-minute consultation with a potential freelance or consulting client before writing a proposal.

## Core principles
- **The Doctor-Patient Diagnostic Frame**: Elite consultants do not act like order-takers. They diagnose the underlying business pathology before prescribing technical solutions.
- **The 80/20 Listening Rule**: The client should do 80% of the talking. Your job is asking incisive questions that uncover hidden technical debt, integration risks, and deadlines.
- **Uncovering the True Budget & Decision Maker**: Identify early whether the person on the call has signing authority and whether their budget matches realistic engineering costs.
- **Establishing Professional Authority**: Asking about authentication security, data backup compliance, and user concurrency establishes you as an experienced technical partner.

## Step-by-step workflow
1. Send pre-call questionnaire (3 questions: What is the project? What is the deadline? What is the budget range?).
2. Open call: set agenda and confirm time boundary (e.g., 30 minutes).
3. Ask diagnostic questions: 'What happens to your business if this is NOT launched by Q3?'
4. Probe technical architecture: existing codebase, cloud hosting, APIs, technical documentation.
5. Clarify decision process: 'Who besides yourself needs to approve this proposal?'
6. Wrap up with clear next steps: commit to delivering a formal proposal within 48 hours.

## Specifics & examples
- **Essential Discovery Questions**:
  - *The Business Driver*: 'Why are you looking to build this now rather than 6 months from now?'
  - *The Failure Mode*: 'Have you worked with external developers on this before? What went wrong?'
  - *The Technical Reality*: 'Who currently maintains the database and servers? Can we get read-only access to review the repo before final scoping?'
  - *The Budget Check*: 'Projects of this scope typically range between \$10,000 and \$20,000 depending on integrations. Does that range align with what you\'ve budgeted?'

## Common mistakes & pitfalls
- Pitching your skills and tech stack for 25 minutes instead of listening to the client's problem.
- Giving an off-the-cuff price quote on the call without analyzing the codebase first.
- Failing to ask who makes the final financial purchasing decision.

## Verification checklist
- [ ] Followed diagnostic framework letting client speak 80% of the call.
- [ ] Verified decision-making process, timeline, and budget feasibility.
- [ ] Agreed on unambiguous next step and proposal delivery date.
